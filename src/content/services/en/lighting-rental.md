## What Is Lighting Rental?

Stage lighting is the art of controlling the intensity, color, and movement of light to create atmosphere and direct the audience's focus. The right lighting can highlight decor, support performances, and amplify the emotion of every moment, whether for a grand stage or an intimate ambience.
