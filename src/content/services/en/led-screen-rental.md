## What Is LED Screen Rental?

An LED screen, or videotron, is a modular display panel that can be assembled into any size your stage requires. Unlike projectors, LED screens stay crisp and visible even under bright lighting or in outdoor settings. Every rental includes the video processor, controller, cabling, and a safe rigging structure.
