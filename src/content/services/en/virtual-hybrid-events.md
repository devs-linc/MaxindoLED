## What Are Virtual & Hybrid Events?

A virtual event is broadcast entirely online, while a hybrid event combines a physical presence with online participation. We manage the entire production workflow, from multi-camera capture, audio processing, and on-screen graphics to broadcast distribution across platforms such as Zoom, YouTube, and your social media channels.
