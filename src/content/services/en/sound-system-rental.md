## What Is Sound System Rental?

A sound system is a chain of audio equipment that turns sound into a strong, clear output, consisting of speakers, power amplifiers, mixers, and microphones. We tailor the configuration and the number of speaker positions to your venue size and audience count so that every word, song, and note is heard perfectly without distortion.
