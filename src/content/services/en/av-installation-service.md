## What Is AV Installation?

AV installation is the permanent fitting of audio visual equipment into a room or building. Unlike rentals, these systems are purpose-built for long-term needs, integrated with the building's infrastructure, and optimized so your staff can use them easily every day.

## Why Choose Maxindo LED?
