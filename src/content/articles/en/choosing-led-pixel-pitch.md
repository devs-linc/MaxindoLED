Pixel pitch is one of the most confusing terms people run into when renting an LED screen. Yet understanding it is the key to making your event visuals look crisp instead of blocky or blurry. This article explains what pixel pitch is and how to choose the right one for your venue and event type.

## What Is Pixel Pitch?

Pixel pitch is the distance between the center of one pixel and the next on an LED panel, measured in millimeters. A P3 panel has 3 mm between pixels, while P5 has 5 mm. The smaller the number, the denser the pixels and the sharper the image looks up close. However, a smaller pixel pitch also means a higher rental cost because the panel packs in far more LED components.

## Pixel Pitch and Viewing Distance

A common industry rule of thumb is that the ideal minimum viewing distance in meters roughly equals the pixel pitch number. So a P3 screen starts to look smooth from about 3 meters, while P5 is ideal from 5 meters and beyond. If your audience sits very close to the panel, such as behind a wedding stage, choose a small pitch like P2 or P2.5 to keep the image clean.

## Recommendations by Event Type

- **Weddings and intimate indoor events**: P2 to P3, since guests are often near the screen.
- **Corporate conferences and seminars**: P3 to P4 for a balance of sharpness and cost.
- **Large indoor concerts**: P3.9 is popular because it is durable and sharp enough for a main screen.
- **Outdoor events and billboards**: P5 to P10 is sufficient since the audience watches from a distance.

## Other Factors to Consider

Beyond pixel pitch, pay attention to brightness, measured in nits. Outdoor screens need at least 5,000 nits to stay visible under sunlight, while indoor screens only need 800 to 1,500 nits. Also consider total resolution: a large screen with a small pitch produces high resolution that is great for displaying detailed text and logos.

## Conclusion

Choosing the right pixel pitch is about balancing viewing distance, visual quality, and budget. The smallest is not always the best.
