This 32-channel Digital Audio Mixer is a reliable control hub for professional
stage production, from band concerts and seminars to worship services and
weddings. Every preamp is digitally controlled, so gain, EQ, compressor, and gate
settings can be saved and recalled (scene recall) instantly when transitioning
from soundcheck to the main event. Eight built-in stereo effects processors
deliver studio-quality reverb, delay, and modulation with no extra hardware
needed.

Wireless control via tablet lets the sound engineer walk the venue and tune the
sound directly from the audience position, ensuring an optimal mix at every spot.
Flexible routing with 16 mix buses and 6 matrix outputs offers the freedom to
handle stage monitors, broadcast, and recording all at once. The Maxindo LED
rental package includes a stage box, multicore cabling, and a supporting
technician when needed. It is the ideal solution for those who demand both
quality and speed when managing many audio sources.
