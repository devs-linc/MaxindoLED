The Speaker Line Array is the backbone of an audio system for large-scale events
that demand powerful yet crystal-clear sound all the way to the back rows. The
vertically flown module configuration produces consistent, even sound projection
across the entire area, reducing dead spots and overly loud zones alike. With
1000 watts per module and a maximum SPL of 135 dB, the system can fill open-air
venues and multipurpose halls with striking sonic energy.

A frequency response of 55 Hz to 20 kHz ensures natural vocal reproduction and
powerful bass impact, ideal for music concerts, festivals, large worship
gatherings, and gala dinners. Each module is active with an internal bi-amp
amplifier, simplifying cabling and speeding up setup. The Maxindo LED rental
package includes companion subwoofers, a processor, rigging, cabling, and an
experienced sound engineer who tunes the system to your venue's acoustics.
Consult us on the number of modules required based on your audience capacity.
