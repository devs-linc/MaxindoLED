The P3.9 outdoor LED screen is engineered to perform at its best under direct
sunlight and challenging weather. With 5500 nits of brightness, the display stays
clearly legible during the day, making it the perfect solution for open-air
concerts, festivals, main stages, and event billboards. An IP65 protection rating
on both the front and rear shields the panels from rain, dust, and high humidity,
so it can run safely all day long.

The 50 x 100 cm cabinets use strong yet lightweight die-cast aluminium, speeding
up both installation and teardown. The 3.9mm pixel pitch keeps imagery clean and
sharp even for audiences in the front rows. The rental package includes a video
processor, power distribution system, cabling, and experienced technicians on
standby throughout the event. Maxindo LED also provides ground-support or hanging
structures to match your stage design. Discuss your dimension and venue
requirements with our team.
