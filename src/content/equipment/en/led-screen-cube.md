The LED Cube Screen is a cube-shaped display installation with five viewing faces
that can show content synchronously from every direction. This three-dimensional
form makes it a stunning focal point for creative stages, exhibition booths,
product launches, and event decor that wants to stand out. Animated content
mapped across the entire surface of the cube creates an illusion of depth and
motion that audiences will not soon forget.

Measuring 100 x 100 x 100 cm with a 3.9mm pixel pitch, the cube is compact enough
to place at the center of a room while staying sharp at close range. Maxindo LED
provides the media server along with multi-face content mapping, so video and
animation flow seamlessly across each surface. The rental package includes
rigging, a processor, technicians, and creative support to prepare content that
matches your event theme. Bring an extraordinary visual element to your event
with the LED Cube from Maxindo LED.
