The Moving Head LED is a versatile stage fixture that combines beam, spot, and
wash functions in a single unit, giving lighting designers tremendous
flexibility. The 230 watt LED source produces a bright, sharp output, while 540
degrees of pan and 270 degrees of tilt enable dynamic beam movement across the
stage. Two gobo wheels and a prism create dazzling patterns and beam-splitting
effects, perfect for concerts, dance performances, clubs, and entertainment
events.

Fully controlled via the DMX-512 protocol with 16 channels, the fixture is easy
to program to follow the tempo of the music or the cues of a show. Its efficient
LED power consumption keeps the unit cool and energy-saving compared to
conventional lamps. Maxindo LED supplies the fixtures in even quantities to build
symmetrical rigs, complete with a lighting controller, DMX cabling, and rigging.
