The PAR LED is a versatile wash light that forms the foundation of lighting for
nearly every type of event. Fitted with 18 RGBW LEDs of 12 watts each, the
fixture can produce thousands of rich, saturated color combinations to color
backdrops, walls, stages, and decor areas. RGBW color mixing makes everything
from warm white to soft pastels achievable, something ordinary RGB lights
struggle with, making it ideal for weddings, gala dinners, and concerts.

Several control modes are available, ranging from precise DMX to automatic mode
and sound-active mode that pulses to the rhythm of the music. Its low power draw
allows many units to be daisy-chained on a single power line without worrying
about overload. Maxindo LED provides PAR LEDs in large quantities complete with
DMX cabling, daisy-chain power, and a controller. They pair perfectly with moving
heads to build a complete lighting rig. Tell us about your event lighting needs
and let our team design the right color scheme.
